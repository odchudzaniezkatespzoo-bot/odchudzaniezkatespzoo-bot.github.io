Privacy Policy — effective 2026-11-11

1. Introduction

Welcome to Emerald Summit! Your privacy is important to us. This Privacy Policy explains how ODCHUDZANIE Z KATE SP Z O O (“we”, “us”, “our”) collects, uses, and protects information in connection with your use of Emerald Summit (“the App”).

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of data when you use the App:

  • Device Information (e.g., device model, operating system, device identifiers)
  • Install attribution data — when you open the App, our attribution provider AppsFlyer receives your device's advertising ID, basic device and app information (model, OS version, App version, language), your IP address (used to derive your country) and the Google Play install referrer. See the Install Attribution section.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected data to:

  • Provide, maintain, and improve the App
  • Diagnose technical issues
  • Ensure security and prevent fraud or abuse
  • Measure which advertising campaigns brought new players to the App, and detect fraudulent installs

4. Data Retention and Deletion

We retain collected data only as long as necessary for the purposes described above, and it is automatically deleted within 90 days. You may also request deletion of your data at any time by contacting us using the details below, and we will process your request promptly.

5. Advertising

Emerald Summit does not display advertising and does not share your data with advertising networks for ad targeting. We do, however, use an install attribution provider to measure which advertising campaigns bring new players to the App — see the Install Attribution section.

6. Install Attribution (AppsFlyer)

Emerald Summit uses AppsFlyer (AppsFlyer Ltd.) to measure how players found the App — for example, which advertising campaign led to an install — and to detect fraudulent installs. AppsFlyer processes the data described in the Information We Collect section on our behalf and only on our instructions. We record installs and app sessions only: no gameplay events, no purchase details and no contact information are sent. The App sends this data when it is opened. You can limit it at any time by resetting or deleting your advertising ID in your device settings, and you can ask us to delete the data held about your device (see the Contact section). AppsFlyer handles this data under the AppsFlyer Services Privacy Policy.

7. Third-Party Services

We may use third-party services for analytics and crash reporting (for example, to measure app performance and fix bugs). These providers process data in accordance with their own privacy policies. We do not sell your personal data.

8. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us using the details below.

9. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for a general audience and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.
